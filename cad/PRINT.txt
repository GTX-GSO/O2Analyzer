==============================================================
Files: body_lower.stl, body_upper.stl, top_cap.stl, bottom_cap.stl

Boards
------
ESP32-C3 OLED     20.5 (Y) × 24.5 (Z) PCB; Type-C on narrow end (OLED/screen side)
ADS1115           25.3 × 9.5 (soldered leads, no headers; floats in −X bay — no cradle)
Type-C protrude   1.0 past outer floor (bottom_cap)
Screen flex       permanently attached to ESP — bottom-load only
Radial +X         wall → screen U-rail → Type-C (outboard of OLED face) → ESP PCB (more central)

Lower bay
---------
Outer height      ledge_z = 32 (bot_t 2 + bay_inner 26 + wire_h 4)
Open bottom       no integral floor; bottom_cap press-fit from below
ESP slots         ±Y full-height guides + PCB top hard stop (esp_stop_h 1.5)
ADS cradle        none (removed — print features fell off)
ESP face_x        ~10.0 (inset 0.7 for ID chord clearance)
Type-C hole       tc_w 9.4 × tc_h 3.7 (snug to shell); tc_x outboard of OLED face
Display U-rail    bottom-load, top stop; rail starts pcb_z0+6; disp_w 13; disp_z0 = 12

Upper / top
-----------
Join sleeve       male 5.0 / female 5.0; join_interfere 0.20; join_male_extra 0.15 (PETG — thicken male only)
Sensor cavity     sensor_h = ao_h_body 25 + sensor_top_clear 4.5 (= 29.5) for top_cap bite
Top cap           friction disc into cavity above AO-02 shoulder; Ø20 for M16
                  disc Ø = cavity Ø30 + top_interfere 0.45 (= Ø30.45); plate_t 2.5

Bottom cap
----------
Solid disc        bot_t 2.0; Type-C stadium 9.4×3.7 on +X; diametral interfere 0.20
Clocking          none — align Type-C to +X display by eye when pressing in
Lead-in           body bottom chamfer 0.8 → engage ~1.2 mm (raise bot_interfere if loose)

Assembly
--------
1. From open bottom of body_lower: slide screen+ESP (flex attached) up —
   screen into U-rail, PCB into ±Y slots — until PCB top hits hard stop
2. Place ADS in −X bay from below (no cradle); route soldered leads upward
3. Press-fit bottom_cap (align Type-C with +X display);
   Type-C tip +1 mm past outer floor
4. Press-fit body_upper onto lower join sleeve
5. Drop AO-02 from top until elec seat / shoulder seats
6. Press-fit top_cap into the 3 mm clear above shoulder —
   M16 protrudes through Ø20 for original cap / tee

If upper join feels loose: raise join_male_extra (now 0.15) toward 0.20–0.25 and reprint lower
If upper join feels tight: lower join_male_extra by 0.05 and reprint lower
If you already printed upper with join_interfere 0.35: reprint upper (interfere back to 0.20) or skip extra on male
If sensor still loose: lower ao_elec_clear / ao_clear and reprint upper
If bottom_cap feels loose: raise bot_interfere slightly or check print shrinkage
If Type-C hole too tight: raise tc_w / tc_h by 0.2 and reprint bottom_cap
If top_cap has no grip: raise top_interfere (now 0.45) toward 0.55–0.60 and reprint top_cap only
If top_cap too tight to press in: lower top_interfere by 0.05–0.10 and reprint
